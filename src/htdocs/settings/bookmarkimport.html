Import bookmarks

Reads a repeater list from a spreadsheet and turns it into bookmarks. Nothing is stored until the preview at the bottom looks right. Accepted formats: .xls, .xlsx and .csv.

6. Can it actually be heard?

Works out what the ground between here and each station does to the signal, using elevation data and the antenna settings under General settings. It is an estimate: it knows the shape of the land, but not buildings, trees, or how much power the far station runs.
